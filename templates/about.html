Our Core Mission

Every semester, thousands of expensive textbooks, scientific calculators, mini-drafters, and lab coats are purchased, used for a few months, and then shelved indefinitely.

CampusShare connects students, faculty, and departments across the college into a circular resource loop. By enabling verified peer-to-peer exchanges and academic note sharing, we lower the cost of education while fostering sustainable campus habits.

Institutional Trust & Safety

Unlike open social media groups or anonymous classifieds, CampusShare operates exclusively within the verified boundaries of our college:

  • Verified Accounts: Students and faculty register with official student ID, department, and semester.
  • Dynamic Trust Scoring: Transparent 0–100 reputation score calculated from verified handovers and peer feedback.
  • Safe On-Campus Handover: All physical exchanges happen at designated campus spots (Library, Cafeteria, Student Center).
Ecosystem Architecture

How CampusShare Works

Five integrated pillars that power our unified campus ecosystem

1. Academic Library Notes

Upload lecture slides, handwritten notes, previous exam question papers, and lab manuals. Peer-reviewed with faculty verification badges.

2. Campus Marketplace

Buy, sell, or donate second-hand calculators, cycles, reference books, and project components with zero middleman commissions.

3. Wanted Request Board

Need a specific book or sensor kit that isn't listed? Post an urgent wanted request with automated smart resource matching.

4. Lost & Found Portal

Lost your ID card, earphones, or umbrella on campus? Report found items with image verification to reunite belongings with rightful owners.

5. Circular Campus Impact

Real-time metrics tracking the total financial savings, carbon emissions prevented, and materials recirculated across the academic year.
