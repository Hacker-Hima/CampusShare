Step-by-Step Flow

The 4-Step Student Trading Lifecycle

Designed for convenience, safety, and transparent pricing

1. List Your Item

Snap a photo, select condition (Brand New → Fair), set your price, or mark it as a Free Giveaway or Barter Exchange.

2. Negotiate & Connect

Interested buyers submit offers or message the seller directly within CampusShare. No need to share personal phone numbers.

3. Safe Handover

Meet on campus at designated safe spots (Central Library, Cafeteria, or Student Center). Inspect the item physically before paying.

4. Earn Trust & Badges

Mark listing as sold to earn +10 campus reputation points, boost your Trust Score, and unlock community badges.

Campus Trading Safety Rules

On-Campus Only

Always arrange pick-up inside college premises during daylight hours.

Pay After Inspection

Verify the condition and functionality of hardware kits before handing over payment.

Report Fraud or Misconduct

Every listing features a direct report button to notify college administrators.
